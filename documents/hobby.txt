A hobby is an activity that you do for fun and relaxation during your free time. It is not your regular job or schoolwork. People choose hobbies because they enjoy them, and hobbies help reduce stress, build new skills, and bring joy to everyday life.

Common Types of HobbiesCreative Arts: Painting, drawing, writing, playing music, or photography.Outdoor Activities: Hiking, gardening, cycling, or bird-watching.Collection and Crafts: Collecting coins or stamps, sewing, or baking.Games and Mind Sports: Playing chess, video games, or reading books.

Why Hobbies MatterThey give your brain a break from work.They help you meet new friends who share your interests.They let you express your inner creativity.They build confidence as you learn and grow.If you would like, tell me what things you like to do or how much free time you have, and I can help you find a new hobby to try!
